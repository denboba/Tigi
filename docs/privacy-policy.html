Nilak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Nilak ("the app") is an unofficial, open-source app built on the Telegram API. It is not affiliated with Telegram. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: bitpyrus@gmail.com.

1. Who processes your data

Nilak is a client for the Telegram network. When you sign in, your account, messages, channels, media and contacts are stored and delivered by Telegram's servers, under Telegram's Privacy Policy. Nilak has no servers of its own that receive your messages, contacts or media. The developer of Nilak cannot read your chats.

3. What Nilak does not do

  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No tracking or analytics SDKs that follow you across apps.
  • No access to your camera, microphone or location unless you use a feature that needs it and grant the permission.

4. Ads

Some public channels show sponsored messages provided by Telegram. Nilak displays them marked as sponsored. They are chosen by Telegram based on the channel, not on your personal data held by Nilak.

5. Security

All traffic between the app and Telegram's servers is encrypted (MTProto). Secret chats are end-to-end encrypted. You can add a passcode and two-step verification in Settings.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on Telegram's servers while your account exists. You can delete messages at any time, and delete your account in the app (Settings → Privacy and Security → Delete my account) or at my.telegram.org. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Children

Nilak is not intended for children under 18 and is not directed at them.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data. For data held by Telegram, use Telegram's tools or contact Telegram. For questions about the app itself, write to bitpyrus@gmail.com.

9. Open source

Nilak's full source code is public at github.com/denboba/Tigi (GNU GPL), so anyone can check how it handles data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
