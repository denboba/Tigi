Child Safety Standards

Nilak · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Nilak has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Accounts, channels and content involved in it are reported and removed, and we cooperate with law enforcement.

1. About Nilak

Nilak is an open-source social app built on the Telegram network. Posts, channels, comments, messages and accounts are stored and delivered by Telegram's servers. Nilak has no servers of its own that hold user content. These standards explain what is prohibited, how users report it, and what we do when we learn of it.

2. What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises minors, including images, videos, drawings, AI-generated or edited material, text and links to such material.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a minor to sexually abuse or exploit them, or asking a minor for sexual images, videos or conversations.
  • Sextortion: threatening to share intimate images of a minor, or using them to demand anything.
  • Trafficking, advertising or soliciting minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, selling, trading or asking for CSAM, or promoting channels or groups that do.
  • Content that normalises or encourages the sexual abuse of children.

Nilak is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

3. How to report in the app

  • A post: tap ⋯ on the post and choose Report, then Child abuse.
  • A channel, group or person: open the profile, tap ⋯ → Report → Child abuse.
  • A message in a chat: long-press it, tap Report, then Child abuse.

In-app reports go to Telegram's moderation team, which can remove the content and ban the accounts and channels involved across the whole network. You can also block a person, and hide a channel from your own Nilak feed (⋯ → Hide).

4. How to report to us

You can also email nilak.safety@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report". Include the link to the post, channel or profile (for example a t.me link) and what you saw. Do not send us the images or videos themselves. We read these reports with priority.

5. What we do

  • We forward the report to Telegram's abuse team (abuse@telegram.org) so the content and accounts are removed at the source.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, and to national authorities or hotlines in the country concerned (for example through the INHOPE network).
  • We respond to valid requests from law enforcement and preserve the information we have, as the law requires.

6. If a child is in danger

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency number first. You can also report directly to:

  • United States: NCMEC CyberTipline, report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678
  • United Kingdom: Internet Watch Foundation, report.iwf.org.uk
  • European Union and other countries: your national hotline at inhope.org

7. Compliance

Nilak complies with applicable child safety laws, including the obligation to report CSAM to the relevant regional and national authorities, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
